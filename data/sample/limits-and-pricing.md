# KiteMetric plans and limits (sample)

| | Starter | Growth | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Events per month | 1 million | 10 million | Unlimited |
| Data retention | 3 months | 13 months | 25 months |
| Seats | 5 | 25 | Unlimited |
| SSO (SAML) | No | Yes | Yes |
| Uptime SLA | None | 99.9% | 99.9% |

- Overage on event limits is billed per additional million events;
  ingestion is never throttled without 30 days notice.
- Prices are in INR for Indian customers and USD elsewhere; the
  current price list is published on the KiteMetric website.
