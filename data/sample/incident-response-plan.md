# KiteMetric incident response plan (sample)

- Incidents are graded P1 (service down or confirmed data breach)
  to P4 (minor, no customer impact).
- A named incident lead is assigned within 1 hour of a P1 being
  declared.
- For a confirmed personal-data breach, affected customers are
  notified within 72 hours of confirmation, with the scope, the
  data categories involved, and the remediation steps.
- Status updates for P1 incidents are posted every 4 hours until
  resolution.
- A written post-incident review is completed within 10 business
  days of a P1 closing.
