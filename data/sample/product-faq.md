# KiteMetric product FAQ (sample)

- Uptime SLA: 99.9% monthly uptime on Growth and Enterprise plans,
  backed by service credits of 10% of the monthly fee per 0.1% below
  target, capped at 50%.
- Support hours: 09:00 to 19:00 IST, Monday to Friday, excluding
  Indian public holidays. P1 incidents are handled 24x7.
- Data export: full event export in CSV from the dashboard, and via
  the REST API, at no extra charge.
- SSO: SAML 2.0 single sign-on on Growth and Enterprise plans.
- Scheduled maintenance windows are announced at least 7 days in
  advance and run between 01:00 and 03:00 IST on Sundays.
