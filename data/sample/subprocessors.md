# KiteMetric subprocessors (sample)

| Subprocessor | Purpose | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amazon Web Services | Hosting and storage | India (Mumbai) |
| Razorpay | Payment processing | India |
| Postmark | Transactional email delivery | United States |
| Sentry | Error tracking | United States |

Notes:

- Sentry is configured to strip personal data from error payloads
  before sending.
- No other subprocessors process customer data as of April 2026.
