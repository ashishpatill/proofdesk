# KiteMetric data retention policy (sample)

- Analytics events are retained for 13 months by default on Growth
  and Enterprise plans, and 3 months on the Starter plan, then
  deleted automatically.
- On contract termination, production customer data is deleted
  within 30 days of the termination date.
- Backups are retained for 90 days and then purged. A deletion from
  production therefore disappears from all backups within 90 days.
- Individual data-subject deletion requests are completed within
  30 days.
- Audit logs are retained for 12 months.
