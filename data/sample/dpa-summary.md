# KiteMetric DPA summary (sample)

- A Data Processing Addendum aligned with India's DPDP Act 2023 is
  available to all paying customers and is signed on request.
- KiteMetric processes personal data only on the customer's
  documented instructions.
- Personal data is never sold, and is never used for advertising or
  model training.
- Customer event data is stored and processed in the AWS ap-south-1
  (Mumbai) region. Two support tools (see the subprocessors list)
  may process limited metadata outside India.
- Subprocessors are announced 30 days before they take effect, and
  customers may object within that window.
