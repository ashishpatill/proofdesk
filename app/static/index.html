Security questionnaires · Claim-checked by design

Questionnaire answers,
checked against the source.

ProofDesk drafts answers from your company's own documents, then checks every claim against the passage it cites before anything ships. Unsupported claims are cut. Uncertain ones go to a human. Nothing is guessed.

Drafter · — Checker · — Decision layer · — Served on AMD Token Factory
1 · Triage

Each question is classified and scored for sensitivity and answerability.

2 · Draft

The drafter writes only from your documents, with a citation on every claim.

3 · Check

Claims scoring ≥ 0.80 ship, ≤ 0.30 are cut, the rest escalate to the checker model.

4 · Review

Anything unsettled lands in a human review queue with the full evidence trail.
