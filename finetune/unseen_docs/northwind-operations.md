# Northwind Analytics — Operations and Support

- Support is available 24/5 (Monday to Friday) by email and chat.
  Phone support is not offered.
- The platform SLA guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime for paid tiers.
- Maintenance windows are announced at least 72 hours in advance.
- Northwind offers a shared Slack channel for enterprise customers.
- Onboarding includes two training sessions for customer teams.
- The free tier is limited to 5 seats and 10 GB of stored data.
- Exports are available in CSV and Parquet formats.
- Northwind's status page publishes incident history for 24 months.
